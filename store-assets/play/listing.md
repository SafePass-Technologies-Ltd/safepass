## Full description (4000 max)

```
SafePass is a monitored road journey service for Nigeria. Before you set off, you register your trip. For the whole journey, a SafePass officer watches your route live from our operations room. If something goes wrong, someone already knows where you are.

This is not a map, and it is not an alarm. It is a person, watching your specific trip.

WHAT A MONITORED JOURNEY GIVES YOU

• A named officer watching your route in real time, from the moment you set off until you arrive
• Your live position on the operations map, so help is never starting from "where were you last?"
• In-app messaging with your officer for the whole journey
• Safety alerts for the road ahead, based on verified incidents reported by travellers and our team
• A panic button that escalates immediately and quietly preserves an audio record of what is happening
• Confirmation of safe arrival, and a full history of every journey you have taken

BUILT AROUND THE ROADS YOU ACTUALLY TRAVEL

Nigeria's intercity corridors carry real risk. SafePass is built for that reality rather than around it.

• Incident reports in plain categories: kidnapping, armed robbery, accident, roadblock, police checkpoint, fake checkpoint, bad road, vehicle breakdown, suspicious activity
• Verified markers along your route, so you can see what is ahead before you reach it
• Journey history you can review afterwards, and share when it matters

WHO IT IS FOR

• Travellers making intercity road journeys who want someone watching, not just an app in their pocket
• Organisations responsible for staff who travel: banks, oil and gas, NGOs, field teams
• Transport operators who want passenger safety to be visible and accountable

Organisations can enrol teams under a subscription, with an admin dashboard for duty-of-care reporting.

WHAT IT COSTS

SafePass is pay-as-you-go per monitored journey. You fund a wallet in the app and each journey is charged from it. There is no free tier, because an officer is watching every journey. The fee is shown before you start, and you are only charged when monitoring begins.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY

Live location is shared with the officer watching your journey and nobody else. Location sharing runs only while a journey is being monitored; it stops when the journey ends. Emergency audio recording happens only after you press the panic button, and you are told about it — and asked to consent — before you can use the app. We never sell your personal information.

Read the full app privacy policy at https://safepass-tech.com/privacy/app

SafePass monitors, alerts, records and escalates. It does not dispatch emergency responders or provide physical security. Our officers watch, respond and coordinate. If you are in immediate physical danger, contact the police.
```

## Deliberate omissions

- **No emoji, no "download now!", no exclamation marks.** The brand tone is
  reassuring, not promotional.
- **No competitor names**, despite the competitive analysis in the spec. Store
  listings that name rivals invite policy review and read as insecure.
- **No claims about response times or outcomes.** The platform does not dispatch
  responders, and the closing paragraph says so plainly.
